# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three audiences share one surface:

- **Omani women leaders and professionals** deciding whether to register. They arrive from social media or word of mouth, often on a phone, and need to see that this is an official national platform and not another private directory before they will submit credentials.
- **Government bodies, private-sector institutions, universities and international organisations** searching for qualified Omani women to nominate to boards, committees, delegations and leadership posts. They need to trust the data and reach it quickly.
- **Researchers, statisticians and policy makers** looking for national indicators on women's participation in leadership.

## Product Purpose

A national platform and database documenting Omani women leaders across every sector, connecting their expertise to leadership opportunities locally, regionally and internationally, and offering accredited training and leadership-development tracks. Success is measured by registrations from credible leaders, by institutions using the database for real nominations, and by the platform being read as an instrument of Oman Vision 2040 rather than a private initiative.

## Brand Personality

Sovereign, credible, forward-looking. The voice is institutional Arabic: declarative, precise, never promotional. Reference points named by the client are the World Economic Forum, Harvard Kennedy School, Women Political Leaders and the World Government Summit. The intended feeling on arrival is national authority, in the client's own framing: LinkedIn plus the United Nations plus Oman Vision 2040.

## Anti-references

- **The previous green Wix site.** Its green palette, mismatched stock illustrations pulled from four different packs, and templated section rhythm are the specific things being replaced. Do not reintroduce clip-art illustration or the green scale.
- **The generic SaaS landing page.** Gradient hero, hero-metric row, three identical feature cards, purple-blue tech gradients. Wrong register for a national institution.

## Design Principles

- **Sovereign, not startup.** Every surface should read as issued by a national body. Weight, restraint and precision over energy and persuasion.
- **Real people, real rooms.** The client supplied photography of actual Omani women in actual meetings. Use it. Never substitute stock imagery or colored blocks for it.
- **Arabic first, not Arabic translated.** RTL is the native direction, not a mirrored afterthought. Arabic display type carries the page.
- **Credibility over claims.** State what the platform holds and does. Do not fabricate document counts, member numbers or partner logos to look established.
- **The phone is the primary screen.** Most leaders will first meet this platform on a phone. Layouts prove themselves at 390px before 1440px.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Body text at or above 4.5:1 contrast; large text at or above 3:1. Verify against the navy and photo-backed bands specifically, where light text on imagery is the likeliest failure.
- Every animation needs a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative. The hero photo reel must hold on a single frame under reduced motion and on small screens.
- Tap targets at or above 44px.
- Photography carries descriptive Arabic alt text; decorative background imagery is marked `alt=""`.
- Colour is never the sole carrier of meaning. The sector hue system is decorative; every sector is also labelled in text.
